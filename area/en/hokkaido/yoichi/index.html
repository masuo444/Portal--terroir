Terroir of Yoichi

Yoichi, Hokkaido

北海道余市町

Of Yoichi's 550 furusato tax items, sake and spirits (137) and seafood (134) form two pillars. In a town with both orchards and the sea, Terroir HUB lists 21 local producers, including wineries and a distillery, alongside 75 rice and vegetable items and 33 fruit items.

Our standards

  • All advertising and sponsored content is labelled "PR".
  • Advertising never changes the order or content of producer listings.
  • Alcohol advertising follows industry self-regulation: nothing that suggests drinking by minors or drink-driving.
  • We review every campaign before accepting it.

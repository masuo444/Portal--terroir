Terroir of Koshu

Koshu, Yamanashi

山梨県甲州市

Sake and wine account for 365 of Koshu's 791 items — nearly half. Terroir HUB lists 32 producers here, more than anywhere else in Yamanashi, followed by 218 craft items and 113 fruit items, reflecting a landscape built around grapes and wine.
